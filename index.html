    An archive you can read in ten years.

    Every recipe gets its own folder. Markdown is for people and notes apps; JSON is for future tools. The source manifest keeps the trail intact.

│   │   ├── metadata.json
│   │   └── image.webp
│   └── 002-soup/ …
├── manifest/
│   ├── sources.md
│   └── recipes.json
└── README.txt
