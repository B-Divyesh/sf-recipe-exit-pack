Privacy policy · updated 5 September 2026

How Recipe Exit Pack handles recipe files

Recipe Exit Pack is designed so the server never needs your recipes, notes, photos, or exported archive.

What the converter processes

Files and text you choose are read by code running in your browser. Image conversion and ZIP creation also happen in your browser. Recipe content is not sent to Recipe Exit Pack, Sociobot, or an AI service.

What is stored

The converter keeps your current work in the open browser tab. The sample is stored separately on this device and never opens your real recipes.

Network and analytics

No advertising, behavioral tracking, third-party fonts, or analytics scripts are included. The offline worker only caches public app files on your device.

Your choices

You can use the converter offline after the first successful visit. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes the offline cache and saved sample changes.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the operator through sociobot.in. Do not include recipe exports in an initial message.
